## YouTube Comment Strategy

**Best Practices:**
1. **Find recently posted videos** (last 7-30 days) - Your comment appears near the top
2. **Add genuine value** - Don't just drop links
3. **Engage with replies** - Build credibility
4. **Like other comments** - Increases your visibility
5. **Target channels** with 10K-500K subscribers (sweet spot for engagement)
6. **Comment within first hour** of video posting for maximum visibility

**Videos to Target:**
- Crypto tutorial videos
- "How to get started" videos
- Exchange comparison videos
- Beginner FAQ videos
- Weekly crypto news videos

**Timing:**
- Most crypto YouTubers post 9am-12pm EST
- Comment within first hour for best placement
- Check these channels daily:
  - Coin Bureau
  - Andrei Jikh
  - Graham Stephan (finance, but covers crypto)
  - BitBoy Crypto
  - Smaller channels (10K-100K subs)

**Don't:**
- ❌ Spam the same comment on multiple videos
- ❌ Comment on old videos (low visibility)
- ❌ Be promotional only (add value first)
- ❌ Reply to yourself to bump your comment (looks desperate)

## Pinterest Strategy Guide

### Account Setup:
1. **Create business account** (not personal) for analytics
2. **Profile name:** "Crypto Tips" or "Bitcoin Guides" (keep anonymous)
3. **Bio:** "Helping beginners navigate cryptocurrency | Latest bonuses & guides"
4. **Link:** https://coinbase-bonus.vercel.app

### Board Setup:
Create these boards (pin your content to multiple relevant boards):
- "Cryptocurrency for Beginners"
- "Bitcoin Guides"
- "Passive Income Ideas"
- "Make Money Online"
- "Side Hustle Tips"
- "Coinbase Tips"

### Posting Strategy:
1. **Create 5-10 pins** with different designs (use templates above)
2. **Post 3-5 pins per day** initially
3. **Space them out** - Pin at 8am, 12pm, 5pm, 9pm
4. **Repin your own pins** to different boards after 1-2 weeks
5. **Add fresh pins monthly** - Pinterest rewards fresh content

### Optimization:
- **Keywords in title:** Include "Coinbase", "Bitcoin", "Crypto", "Free Money"
- **Hashtags:** Use 5-10 relevant hashtags
- **Description:** 200-500 characters, keyword-rich
- **Link:** Always link to your site

### Timeline:
- Week 1-2: Pins start getting impressions
- Month 1-2: Pins gain traction
- Month 3+: Consistent traffic (Pinterest is long-term)

### Best Times to Pin:
- 8-11pm EST (when most users browse)
- Weekends (higher engagement)
- Evening hours weekdays

### Pro Tips:
1. **Create templates in Canva** - Reuse the same design with different text
2. **Video pins** perform better (but static works too)
3. **Test different designs** - See what gets clicks
4. **Pinterest ranks pins for YEARS** - One good pin = long-term traffic
5. **Don't just promote** - Mix in other helpful crypto pins (curate content)

## Creating Pins in Canva (Free)

1. Go to Canva.com (sign up free)
2. Click "Create a design" > "Pinterest Pin"
3. Search templates for "Bitcoin" or "Money"
4. Customize text with your message
5. Download as PNG
6. Upload to Pinterest

**Simple template structure:**
- Top: Eye-catching headline
- Middle: 3-5 bullet points or numbers
- Bottom: Call to action ("Learn More" or "Read Guide")
- Background: Related image (money, bitcoin, laptop, etc.)

No design skills needed - Canva templates look professional!
