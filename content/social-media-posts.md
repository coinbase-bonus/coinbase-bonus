## How to Use These

**Twitter/X:**
- Post 2-3 times per day
- Use relevant hashtags (2-5 max)
- Engage with replies
- Retweet crypto news and add your commentary
- Best times: 9am, 12pm, 3pm EST

**Instagram:**
- Post 1 time per day max
- Use 10-20 hashtags
- Stories: Share crypto tips daily
- Reels: Repurpose TikTok content
- Best time: 11am-1pm EST

**TikTok:**
- Post 1-2 times per day
- Use trending sounds when possible
- Keep videos under 60 seconds
- Add captions (many watch without sound)
- Best times: 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-10pm EST

**Facebook:**
- Post in relevant groups, not your personal profile
- 1-2 posts per day max across all groups
- Longer posts perform well on Facebook
- Join groups like "Cryptocurrency Beginners", "Side Hustles", "Make Money Online"

**General Tips:**
- Create anonymous accounts, separate from personal
- Don't spam - provide value first
- Engage with comments to build credibility
- Mix in non-promotional content (crypto tips, news, etc.)
- Track what works and do more of that
